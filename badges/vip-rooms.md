# VIP Rooms & Token-Gated Access

VIP Room Badges grant accounts entry into exclusive, spam-free communication channels across the Web3 Outpost ecosystem.

---

## 🔒 Overview & Access Mechanics

VIP Badges are earned by locking **SPEC tokens** (`0x6AcD...18BA`) into protocol lockup vaults. Time-bound token commitment guarantees high-quality, aligned discourse without sybil manipulation.

### Access Tiers

* **🥇 Gold VIP Badge (Badge ID: 16):** Highest tier access. Direct core team AMA channels, leadership working groups, and priority alpha discussions.
* **🥈 Silver VIP Badge (Badge ID: 15):** Mid-tier access. Specialized research workstreams and regional networking rooms.
* **🥉 Bronze VIP Badge (Badge ID: 14):** Entry-tier access. General VIP discussion channels and token-locked rooms.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Reference: SPEC Lock Tiers

| Badge Name | Badge ID | SPEC Lock Threshold | Minimum Lockup Period | Transferability |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Bronze VIP Badge** | `14` | 1,000 SPEC | 30 Days | Non-Transferable (Locked) |
| **Silver VIP Badge** | `15` | 10,000 SPEC | 90 Days | Non-Transferable (Locked) |
| **Gold VIP Badge** | `16` | 100,000 SPEC | 180 Days | Non-Transferable (Locked) |

*Note: Tokens remain locked in smart contracts. Withdrawing tokens automatically burns the associated VIP Badge.*
