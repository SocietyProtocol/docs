# Governance & Core Badges

Governance and Core Badges represent operational responsibility, voting weight in the meritocratic DAO, and strategic oversight within Society Protocol.

---

## 🏛️ DAO Governance Power Split

The SP DAO governance power is distributed meritocratically between execution and strategic guidance:

* **Core Team (~38% Voting Power):** Core Team Badges (`25`) grant execution leadership to full-time researchers, engineers, and operational stewards.
* **Affiliates & Partners (~62% Voting Power):** Advisor Badges (`24`) and Partner Community Badges grant strategic oversight to accredited advisors and partner communities.

---

## 🎖️ Badge Showcase & Roles

### 🏛️ DAO Badge (ID: 11)
![DAO Badge](../SP%20badges%20images/SP%20DAO.png)
* **Description:** Issued to official SP DAO community members. Grants forum access and proposal feedback rights.

### 🛡️ Security Council Badge (ID: 12)
![Security Council Badge](../SP%20badges%20images/Security%20Council.png)
* **Description:** Awarded to elected members of the Security Council. Grants emergency multisig keyholder privileges and circuit-breaker controls.

### 📜 Governor Badge (ID: 13)
![Governor Badge](../SP%20badges%20images/Governor.png)
* **Description:** Awarded to meritocratically appointed Governors who execute executive DAO voting power and protocol stewardship.

### 👔 Advisor Badge (ID: 24)
![Advisor Badge](../SP%20badges%20images/Advisor.png)
* **Description:** Awarded to strategic advisors, industry experts, and accredited partner institutions providing long-term guidance.

### ⚡ Core Team Badge (ID: 25)
![Core Team Badge](../SP%20badges%20images/Core%20Team.png)
* **Description:** Awarded to core protocol researchers, developers, and operators following 6 months of continuous full-time contribution.

### 🛠️ Contributor / Builder Badge (ID: 26)
![Contributor Badge](../SP%20badges%20images/Contributor.png)
* **Description:** Awarded to open-source developers, researchers, and community builders who merge research, code, or architecture contributions.

### 🛡️ Moderator Badge (ID: 28)
![Moderator Badge](../SP%20badges%20images/moderator.png)
* **Description:** Issued to community operators and moderators managing Web3 Outpost forums and official communication channels.
