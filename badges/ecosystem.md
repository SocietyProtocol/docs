# Ecosystem & Partner Badges

Ecosystem & Partner Badges distinguish partner communities, ICO fundraiser participants, and third-party Web3 protocols using Society Protocol's identity layer.

---

## 🎖️ ICO Participant Badge

### 🎟️ ICO Participant Badge (Badge ID: 27)
![ICO Participant Badge](../SP%20badges%20images/ICO%20participant.png)
* **Description:** Awarded to all accounts participating in the initial SPEC ICO fundraiser event (Dutch auction). Grants permanent early backer status and verifiable history across future Web4 SP instances.

---

## 🌐 Partner Community Tiers

Partner communities commit to the Society Protocol movement's ideology in exchange for official recognition, UI feature prioritization, and tier badges:

* **Gold Partner Badge:** Top-tier partner communities with direct feature feedback pipelines and full Web3 Outpost UI enclave integration.
* **Silver Partner Badge:** Mid-tier partner communities with specialized working group affiliation.
* **Bronze Partner Badge:** Associated community partners.
