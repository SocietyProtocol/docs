# Core Team & Advisors Directory

This directory details official verification mechanisms for Society Protocol core team members and strategic advisors.

---

## 🛡️ Verifying Official Team & Advisors

To prevent phishing and impersonation, Society Protocol uses on-chain badge verification. **Do not trust social media handles alone.**

An individual is officially recognized **only if** their verified wallet address holds an official badge on the `SocietyProtocolBadges` proxy (`0x2313C0cDdc233c92d16c2cfE17DF5fDCcE556763`):
* **Core Team Badge (ID: 25):** Full-time core researchers, developers, and operators.
* **Advisor Badge (ID: 24):** Recognized strategic advisors.
