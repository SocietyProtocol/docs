# Community & Contributor Badges

This page covers official badges awarded to individual community members, active contributors, early financial backers, and creative competition winners across the Society Protocol ecosystem.

---

## Contributor Badge (ID: 26)

The **Contributor Badge** is awarded to dedicated community members who actively contribute to the Society Protocol movement through research, content, organizing, ecosystem support, or technical initiatives. 

Rather than requiring full-time software engineering, this badge recognizes high levels of alignment, consistent engagement, and meaningful effort in helping the protocol grow.

![Contributor Badge](../SP%20badges%20images/Contributor.png)

### Purpose & Discord Access

A primary utility of the Contributor Badge is granting access to an exclusive, **token-gated channel in the official Society Protocol Discord**.

The goal of this channel is to cultivate a focused, low-noise environment for higher-level discussion, strategic feedback, and direct coordination with core contributors and ambassadors without the clutter of public channels.

### How to Unlock the Private Discord Channel

If you hold a Contributor Badge in your wallet, you can claim your role in Discord using the **Collab.Land** bot:

1. **Join the Discord:** Make sure you are in the official [Society Protocol Discord server](https://discord.gg/societyprotocol).
2. **Locate the Verification Channel:** Navigate to `#collabland-join` (or `#role-verify`).
3. **Connect Your Wallet:** Click on the **"Let's go!"** button provided by the official Collab.Land bot.
4. **Sign the Message:** Follow the unique link to open the Collab.Land interface in your browser and connect the Ethereum wallet that holds your Contributor Badge. Sign the gasless signature to prove ownership.
5. **Access Granted:** Once verified, Collab.Land automatically grants you the `@Contributor` role in Discord, unlocking access to the private contributor channel immediately.

> [!NOTE]
> The Contributor Badge is **Soulbound** (non-transferable). Only the wallet address that originally received the badge can claim the Discord role.

## Early Backers: ICO Participant Badge (ID: 27)

The **ICO Participant Badge** honors early financial supporters who participated in the initial SPEC token fundraiser event (Dutch auction).

![ICO Participant Badge](../SP%20badges%20images/ICO%20participant.png)

- **Badge ID:** `27`
- **Transferability:** Soulbound
- **Perks & Significance:**
    - Provides permanent on-chain proof of early belief and capital commitment to the Society Protocol vision from Day 1.
    - Serves as a key historical credential queried by future Web4 SP instances to grant starting **Genesis Energy** bonuses to founding community backers.

## Community Awards & Contests

Society Protocol regularly hosts community competitions, bounties, and creative hackathons to showcase community talent.

### Creative Contest Winner Badge

The **Creative Contest Winner Badge** is an official recognition badge awarded to participants who win or place in community creative initiatives, including:

- Visual design, art, and meme competitions
- Community writing and deep-dive article contests
- Video essays, animations, and educational media

This badge stands as a permanent tribute in your Outpost profile, verifying your creative contribution to the culture and lore of Society Protocol.
