# Community Partners

Partner communities commit to the Society Protocol movement's ideology in exchange for official recognition, UI feature prioritization, and tier badges on the Web3 Outpost.

---

## What is a Community Partner?

Society Protocol is designed to foster a decentralized federation of aligned groups, projects, and network state experiments. A Community Partner is an established Web3 community, DAO, or organization that has formally aligned with the Society Protocol movement.

Rather than operating in isolation, partner communities deploy badges on the Outpost, coordinate shared initiatives, and establish early relationships that will carry forward into upstart Web4 instances.

---

## Partner Community Tiers

Partnership tiers reflect levels of organizational integration, collaborative depth, and ecosystem commitment:

| Tier | Badge | Recognition & Perks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | **Gold Partner Badge** | Top-tier partner communities. Receive direct feature feedback pipelines with the core development team and full Web3 Outpost UI enclave integration. |
| **Tier 2** | **Silver Partner Badge** | Mid-tier partner communities with specialized working group affiliation and joint research / governance initiatives. |
| **Tier 3** | **Bronze Partner Badge** | Formally associated community partners recognized across the Outpost ecosystem. |

---

## Role in Web4 Genesis Instances

When independent Web4 Society Protocol instances launch in the future, founders can query the Web3 Outpost to invite partner community members directly as founding actors, granting them starting Energy or specialized rights based on their verified community partner affiliation.
