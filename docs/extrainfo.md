Features
The Web3 Outpost application is foundational in transitioning the world from Nation States/Web3 into Synchronized States/Web4.
The features it contains to accomplish this are:
Accounts: A sybil-resistant account system, accessible to any user in our movement, built on top of Ethereum. Accounts
All users are welcome to create an account when they join the Society Protocol movement. The account is meant to stay with you over time.
As we move forward on this journey, each account will accumulate a unique history––consisting of badges and SPEC tokens––showing its unique identity.
These same identities, will later be used to transition into Society Protocol instances, starting when the MVP is developed (3–6 years).
The account system will remain active for many decades. After the MVP is completed, many new Web4 SP instances will spawn from the Web3 account system every year.
The account system helps all communities and participants who are part of the SP movement to establish a singular history...so that all participants and communities can transition directly into SP instances with their accumulated histories fully in-tact.
Accounts have a referral system, which they can use to create a permanent relationship chain between their accounts. Later on, this chain can be seen and used to create SP instances in a way acknowledges these relationships. (The referral feature is meant to imitate an infantile version of the Parenting Value Function (VF).)
Participants can create as many accounts as they desire, but we suggest only one account per person––allowing it to accumulate a real history of identity over time via badges––which will then transition together with its contributions and characteristics into SP instances.
Badges
Badges are NFTs attached to accounts, they are flexible by design—serving either as functional credentials that unlock specific permissions, or as permanent attributions that create a verifiable record of an account's contributions and characteristics. Badges are what differentiates accounts over time.
a badge example picture
Anyone is able and welcome to create badges. When the Society Protocol DAO creates a badge, it is considered an "official" badge. When a community creates a badge, it is considered a badge of that community. Each badge is
differentiated by: who created it, what it signifies, and what it controls.
Badges can support a wide range of qualities...including verifiable qualities, by using data from smart contracts and zkTLS. Some examples include:
Communities can (manually) award badges to those who have attended their in-person conferences or pop-up cities
Badges can be used to verify important information such as countries of origin, financial records, and age (by using zkTLS)
Communities can award badges to those who have bought and used an ENS name specifically from their domain (via smart contracts)
Badges can be used to verify social media accounts of influencers with a certain unique follower, amount of followers, or created before a certain date (by using zkTLS)
All of these badges can then be used by communities to grant special privileges to accounts, such as: discounts on network state residency, access to exclusive token gated chat channels, allowing entry only for individuals from a specific country of origin, moderator access or status hierarchy signifiers within their communities, etc...
Much of the value of badges comes from communities which are intending to launch Synchronized State instances awarding badges to segment for their intended audience in meaningful ways. We encourage our movement's participants to identify characteristics which they believe will be desirable account qualities in their Synchronized State instances, and create badges for those contributions or characteristics––awarding them to appropriate accounts.
Badges are represented by ERC-1155 NFTs on Ethereum.
Each badge has four permissions: mint (who can mint this badge), burn (who can burn this badge), transfer (who can transfer this badge), and manage (who can edit this badge).

Web3 Outpost (Schelling Point)
The Web3 Outpost will serve as the Schelling Point around which the Society Protocol movement will gather. It’s purpose is to keep our community aligned and together through this journey, by using the censorship-resistant, “immutable”, and economic qualities of Web3; as we build the transition to Web4. (Don’t worry…this won’t be a quick transition, and we expect our Web3 Outpost to still be around and valuable in 20 years time).

Ultimately, the Web3 Outpost is designed to: bind us together, keep track of individual contributions, provide an economic substrate to finance and manage our organization, and provide a censorship-resistant space for hosting our community and content.

The Web3 Outpost consists of several pieces:
Society Protocol DAO9 – The DAO serves as the decision-making body and treasury for our movement. It's governed exclusively by Governors—badge-holding members from the core team, affiliates, and our community with specialized expertise and skin in the game10.

Unlike typical token-based DAOs, ours deliberately excludes pseudonymous accounts and SPEC token holders from direct governance11. This design prevents sybil attacks and upholds our meritocratic principles by ensuring only identified, accountable parties make decisions. Governors may add or remove peers, but governance always remains with proven contributors rather than anonymous participants12.

Web3 Application – The Web3 Application serves to provide unique identity accounts for all participants in our movement. It tracks the contributions and characteristics of each individual account over time by using the badge system.

Badges are ERC-1155 NFTs on Ethereum. They can represent any type of contribution or characteristic:

Governor badges represent the ability to make decisions in the DAO
A VIP access badge represents access to the VIP communication channels
Profile badges represent the characteristics of the account holder, with an avatar and bio information
zkTLS badges can link to accounts real-world identities, social media accounts, identity, and financial records
Partner community badges represent communities officially affiliated with Society Protocol movement
Badges are a way to accumulate identity, they serve to distinguish accounts. The Web3 Application allows anyone to create accounts and badges. The Society Protocol DAO has a set of official badges to represent contributions, access roles, and characteristics. How these badges are interpreted is in the “eye of the beholder.” Each community in the SP movement will decide for itself which badges it values.

Upon the initial release of the SP MVP and thereafter…communities will use their unique accounts (with accumulated identity histories, badges, and SPEC) from the Web3 Outpost to create and portal identities into their unique Web4 Society Protocol instances. Millions of unique Web4 instances are expected to be launched in this process, because each community will want their own instance. The evolution of SP instances, and the communities launching them will stretch over decades, if not centuries. As a constant stream of new communities launch instances, they will create a feedback loop, which will refine the protocol with each new instance launched over time.

The Web3 Application additionally allows accounts to form and cluster inside of communities. These communities consist of: our partner communities and unaligned communities13. They represent different groups that are gathering at the Web3 Outpost, with the intention of transferring their communities into Society Protocol instances. The Web3 Application allows these communities to gather in one spot, keep track of members and contributions via badges, interact using their censorship resistant identities, and make decisions as a group.

All sorts of communities—both established organizations and new upstarts—will want to create SP instances to coordinate themselves. These instances provide complete societal infrastructure out of the box: shared reality, identity systems, monetary systems, governance frameworks, synchronized history, and organizational capacity—all designed to scale seamlessly from 2 people to 10 billion. The purpose of the Web3 Application/Outpost is to enable these groups a clearly defined, accurate set of Genesis Actors based on contributions and characteristics to enable them to start the most potent and powerful SP instances immediately14.

Website – The Society Protocol website hosts all the content and information about the project. It’s a static website with built in censorship-resistance, which is hosted on distributed platforms, such as IPFS / Filecoin, in addition to a traditional DNS.

SPEC Token – The SPEC token serves as a representation of contribution to Society Protocol. It represents value stored, to be transferred by willing communities into Energy as they start Society Protocol instances. The SPEC token is crucial for us to keep track of contributions, finance the project, and align economic incentives while we build Society Protocol.

SPEC serves as your tickets into the new world. When communities go to start SP instances, they will use a combination of SPEC tokens & account system badges to determine who to include (based on accounts) and how much Energy to give them (based on SPEC) in their instances.

Millions of Society Protocol instances are expected to be created—with SPEC token holders having a chance to receive an airdrop of Energy in each one of these instances. Communities aren’t forced to grant SPEC token holders Energy, it’s rather that the incentives naturally align.

As communities look to fill the ranks of their SP instances with valuable members— they will look to grant initial Energy distributions to accounts which have shown a legitimate history of participating and contributing to the SP movement—SPEC token holders.

SPEC serves as a way to coordinate the creation of potentially grandiose SP instances based on legitimate contribution right away. It provides a legitimate process for the distribution of initial Energy, which is (generally) going to be the most optimal, fair, and efficient way for most SP instances (not all) to establish themselves. These instances will retain a direct line of legitimacy, and be able to scale participation from day one by using the account system (to decide who to invite) and SPEC token holdings (to decide how much Energy to give each account)15.
