# Governance & the SP DAO

The Society Protocol movement is not a democracy; it is a **meritocracy**. Governance is designed to compress the highest and most aligned expertise possible—from our core team and deeply experienced affiliates—to make aligned, optimal group decisions while bypassing the sybil-vulnerabilities of traditional Web3 token-voting.

---

## Meritocratic Architecture

The **Society Protocol DAO** serves as the decision-making body and treasury for the movement:

- **Governed Exclusively by Governors:** The DAO is governed by holders of the [Governor Badge (ID: 13)](https://app.societyprotocol.io/badges/13)—meritocratically appointed members from the core team and affiliates (strategic advisors and partner communities) who possess specialized expertise and direct skin in the game.
- **Exclusion of Pseudonymous Accounts & SPEC:** Unlike typical token-based DAOs, pseudonymous accounts and raw SPEC token holdings are deliberately excluded from direct execution voting. This prevents economic treasury capture, plutocracy, and sybil manipulation.
- **Accountable Peer Management:** Governors may add or remove peers based on proven contributions and alignment, ensuring decision-making power remains with identifiable, accountable stewards.

---

## The Role of SPEC Holders: Parallel Feedback

While the SPEC token does not confer direct executive voting power, community perspective is vital:

- **[Snapshot Parallel Feedback Space](https://snapshot.box/#/s:spec.societyprotocol.eth):** SPEC token holders can vote on proposals in a parallel decision-making structure via Snapshot.
- **Sentiment & Mindset Gauge:** This space gives Governors direct, unfiltered insight into community sentiment, perceptions, and intentions without encumbering token holders with the operational and legal overhead of protocol execution.
- **Modular Decision Delegation:** In future phases, Governors may elect to delegate specific modular decision-making power to SPEC holders on a per-decision basis.

## Official Governance Badges

All official governance badges are soulbound ERC-1155 tokens on Ethereum Mainnet:

| Badge | ID | Role & Description |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **DAO Badge** | `11` | Official entity marker representing the SP DAO account itself (`societyprotocol.eth`). |
| **Security Council Badge** | `12` | Emergency multisig keyholder privileges and circuit-breaker controls held by the Security Council. |
| **Governor Badge** | `13` | Meritocratic DAO voting seat. Awarded to core team leaders and key partner affiliates to execute proposals. |
| **Advisor Badge** | `24` | Strategic advisors, industry experts, and accredited partner institutions providing long-term guidance. |
| **Core Team Badge** | `25` | Awarded to core protocol researchers, developers, and operators following continuous full-time contribution. |
| **Contributor Badge** | `26` | Active contributors across research, community organizing, content, or ecosystem development. |
| **Moderator Badge** | `28` | Community operators managing Outpost forums and official communication channels. |

> **Community-Level Governance:**  
> The governance documented above applies strictly to the global Society Protocol DAO. Individual communities hosted on the Outpost configure their own internal decision-making frameworks within their specific community dashboard at [app.societyprotocol.io/communities](https://app.societyprotocol.io/communities).
